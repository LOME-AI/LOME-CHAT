Buttons are centered in the card; the disclosure is two centered lines; the download bar is the shared animated TTS download bar (one component with the accessibility widget's audio section) and sits above the controls. On this page ▸

Most AI chat apps treat your conversations as their data. We built HushBox around the opposite assumption.

Every message is encrypted on your device before it ever leaves, and the keys never touch our servers. That single constraint shapes everything else about how the product works.

It also means features like this one, reading a page aloud, have to run locally too.

The highlighted sentence is the current chunk (CSS Custom Highlight API; whole-paragraph tint as fallback). Auto-scroll keeps it in view; Esc stops from anywhere. The card scrolls away with the header; Stop remains reachable via Esc or scrolling back.
