# Copy Deck — "A Tour of HushBox HQ" (LOCKED)

The single source of truth for every word in the ad. Overlay text and VO are
the same words. Change nothing here without re-checking VO takes, overlays, and
the timing map together.

**Voice:** flat, dry, unhurried, faintly amused. A museum guide who has given
this tour a thousand times and still finds one thing funny about it. Never
announces, never asks, never sells.

**Beats:** every "—" is ~0.8s of silence. The beat is where the joke lives; do
not rush it.
